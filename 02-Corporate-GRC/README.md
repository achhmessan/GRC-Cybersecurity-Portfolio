# Corporate GRC Projects

This section documents hands-on Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) projects focused on corporate cybersecurity compliance and security assurance.

## Frameworks and Standards
- SOC 2 Type II and AICPA Trust Services Criteria
- ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- Enterprise Risk Management
- Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)

## Planned Projects
1. SOC 2 Type II Readiness Assessment
2. ISO 27001 Gap Assessment
3. Internal Security Audit
4. Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment
5. Security Control Testing and Evidence Collection
6. Audit Findings and Remediation Tracking
7. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Assessment

All projects use fictional organizations, simulated audit evidence, and authorized lab environments for educational purposes.
